First Run Notes

First run used FOC and low current to smooth out power delivery. Got to 39 mph before the lights stopped.

It has a 2500 W MAC motor (4.9 kg) and 52 V 17 Ah battery. Field Oriented Control gives ultra-smooth Tesla-like acceleration.

Most 40+ mph e-bikes or heavy moped-style builds weigh 70 to 100+ lbs. This delivers 40 mph performance in an ultra-lightweight 40 lb frame.

Tesla-style Field Oriented Control algorithm gives great smooth power delivery. Gemini AI was very impressed — AI says this is a new most efficient transportation system. 40 lb / 40 mph / 40 mi range carrying a 200+ lb load.

Originally limited battery current to 45 A ? power fluctuations and early VESC cutouts (high speed but no torque). Raised max amp to 65 A after AI advice.
